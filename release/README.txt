JAMP - Just Another Music Player (portable)

Run portable.exe. Nothing to install.

Settings, your playlist and the lyrics/cover cache are kept in a "jamp-data" folder
next to portable.exe, so you can carry the whole folder on a USB stick.

Optional: put ffmpeg.exe on your PATH (or next to portable.exe) to also play Opus, WMA,
APE, WavPack, DSD, AC3 and other formats the built-in decoders don't cover.

Keys: Space play/pause, Left/Right seek 5s, Up/Down volume, N next, B previous,
Enter play selected, Delete remove selected, Ctrl+O open files.
